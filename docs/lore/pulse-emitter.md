# The Pulse Emitter

The weapon every Warden carries. Shown in game as **Warden Archive — Entry 008** (always unlocked).

## Archive text (in game)

> Coherent light is the one signal the Chromatics cannot swallow. They are stolen frequency, scattered and hungry. A beam where every wave moves in step hits them like a wrong note, and the lock that holds them together breaks.
>
> Nobody manufactures Pulse Emitters. The first was built in the Basement from decommissioned hardware. Its schematic has been copied by hand ever since, and every Warden builds their own.
>
> No two match. Dex's runs hot around a magnetron pulled from a microwave. Juno's is a tight coil of tuned antenna. Both fire the same clean white.
>
> When a Fragment is pulled back, its signal doesn't leave. It settles into the emitter's resonance and stays. The device remembers everyone it has saved.

## How it works in the game

- Fires white shots of coherent light (`#FFFFFF`, faint `#E8F0FF` trail).
- Rescued Warden Fragments are "woven into" it. That is the in-world reason upgrades and classes carry between runs.
- Worn on the **right forearm**, not held. A bracer reads from all four sprite directions without redrawing a gun per direction, and shots leave from the lens at the wrist.
- Five small sockets on top of the housing light up as Fragments are recovered (shown in the archive schematic).

## Canon notes — not shown in game yet

**Null leads the Wardens.** He is the network technician from the design doc who rode out the Collapse in the Faraday-shielded server basement and built the first Pulse Emitter there. Dex and Juno are two of the people helping him.

- He is the voice on the channel: the `[LEADER]` in the intro script.
- **No face, no portrait, and no on-screen name yet.** He appears only as a signal icon and a voice. The archive deliberately doesn't credit the first emitter's builder.
- His colour is **white**. It matches the white coherent light every emitter fires, so the weapon's colour quietly points back to him.
- "My basement" in the intro script's call scene is the only hint, and it can stay.
- Reveal later: the schematic everyone copies is his, and the Basement is where it began.
